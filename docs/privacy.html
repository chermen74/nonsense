Privacy Policy

Nonsense · last updated 22 August 2026

Nonsense collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDK in the app beyond the store billing library described below.

What stays on your device

Everything you make and every setting you choose is stored on your device alone and never leaves it:

  • the bumper table you arrange, and each bumper's colour and shape;
  • your ink, tone, translucency, screen tint, canvas and haptic setting;
  • the ball's size and shape, and whether catching is on;
  • whether the unlock is active on this install.

Paintings and etched lightning are held in memory only and are gone when the app closes. Nothing is uploaded, backed up to us, or synchronised between devices — there is nowhere for it to go.

Network

The app makes no network requests of its own. It has no server and there is no service behind it.

The Google Play Billing Library (Android) and StoreKit (iOS) are part of the app so that a subscription can be bought and checked. Those components communicate with Google Play and Apple respectively, and that communication is governed by Google's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy. Because of them, the app declares internet and network-state permissions; the app itself never uses them.

Payment

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card, your billing address, or your name. The store tells the app one thing only: whether a subscription is currently active. No purchase identifier is transmitted anywhere by us.

Children

Nonsense is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. The history of this page is public, in the repository the app is built from.
