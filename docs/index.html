Nonsense

Something to do with your hands.

A fidget toy. Throw a ball and let it ring, spin a knurled dial, bounce through a table of bumpers, throw lightning that spreads and stays etched where it struck, or let the ball leave ink wherever it goes. No scores, no account, and nothing collected. Sound if you want it, and off until you ask.

Open the toy

It runs in the browser, needs nothing installed, and keeps what you set on the device you set it on. On a phone, Add to Home Screen gives it its own icon and the whole screen.
