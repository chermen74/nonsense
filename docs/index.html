Nonsense

Something to do with your hands.

A fidget toy. Throw a ball and let it ring, spin a knurled dial, bounce through a table of bumpers, throw lightning that spreads and stays etched where it struck, or let the ball leave ink wherever it goes. No scores, no account, and nothing collected. Five voices to play it in, or none.

Open the toy

It runs in the browser, needs nothing installed, and keeps what you set on the device you set it on. On a phone, Add to Home Screen gives it its own icon and the whole screen.

On an Android phone

Download the app (.apk)

The real app rather than the page: proper haptics, and it works with no signal. It is rebuilt from the latest commit every time one lands. Android will ask twice before it installs — once to allow installs from your browser, and once because Play Protect does not recognise a build that did not come from the Play Store. Both are expected: this is signed with a throwaway debug key, which is also why it cannot talk to Play about a subscription. On the paywall, tap have a code? and enter the code from the README to open everything.
