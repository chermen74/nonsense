Nonsense

Something to do with your hands.

A fidget toy. Throw a ball and let it ring, spin a knurled dial, bounce through a table of bumpers, throw lightning that spreads and stays etched where it struck, or let the ball leave ink wherever it goes. No scores, no sounds, no account, and nothing collected.
