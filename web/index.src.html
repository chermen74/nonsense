Nonsense

Drag anywhere and let go to flick. On bolt that flick throws lightning: it spreads, strikes a wall, and stays etched there in the ink you threw it with — clear wipes the scene. Turn on catch and you have to land on the ball to grab it. Open palette for thirty-six inks, five levels of translucency, seven canvases to sit on, and the screen tint. On the bumper table, Edit lets you add, delete, move, recolour, reshape — outlines or any letter — and pull each one out of shape. Menu goes back to the front door — so does Esc.
